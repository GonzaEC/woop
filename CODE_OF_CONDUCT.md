# Code of Conduct

Woop follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on their GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.
